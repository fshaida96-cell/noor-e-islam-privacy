Noor-e-Islam: Privacy Policy

App: Noor-e-Islam: Namaz, Azan, Quran & Qibla · Package: com.noorislam.app

Effective date: 22 October 2026

In short: Noor-e-Islam works fully without an account. Your location stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and shares some device data with Google to show and measure ads. We do not use analytics of our own and never sell your data. Only if you choose to log in, your name, email and your bookmarks, favourite duas and tasbeeh counts are saved to your account so they can sync between devices.

1. Who we are

Noor-e-Islam ("the app", "we") is an Islamic app for prayer times, azan reminders, Quran reading and recitation, duas, tasbeeh and Qibla direction. For any privacy question, contact us at fshaida96@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Location (GPS, or the city you choose from the built-in list) is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to our servers or to any third party.
  • Settings (language, calculation method, azan preferences, font size and so on), last-read position, bookmarks, favourite duas and tasbeeh history are stored locally on your phone.
  • Azan and reminders are scheduled on your phone and work offline.

3. Information saved only if you create an account (optional)

Login is optional. If you sign up with email and password or sign in with Google, we use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, provided by Google LLC) to store:

DataPurpose
Name, email address, profile photo link (Google sign-in only), app languageTo create and manage your account
Account creation and last login timeAccount management and security
Quran bookmarks, favourite duas, daily tasbeeh countsTo sync your data between devices

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Internet services the app uses

  • Quran recitation and translation audio are streamed from everyayah.com the first time you play an ayah, then cached on your phone. Like any website, that server receives your IP address when your phone downloads the audio. No account information is sent.
  • Announcements may be read from our Firebase database to show occasional notices on the home screen. No personal data is sent for this.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ads are never shown on the Quran reading, Qibla or prayer-time screens. To show ads, the AdMob software inside the app collects and shares the following data with Google:

Data shared with GooglePurpose
Advertising ID of your deviceShowing ads, limiting how often an ad repeats, personalised ads (only with your consent where required)
IP address and approximate location derived from itShowing ads suitable for your region, preventing fraud
Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size) and app informationDelivering and displaying ads correctly
Ad interactions (ads seen and tapped)Measuring ads, reporting and preventing fraud
Diagnostic data (ad loading errors, performance)Keeping ads working reliably

We do not send Google your GPS location, your name, your email or any of your Quran, dua or tasbeeh data for advertising. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before ads are shown, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in the app: More → Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking or profiling of our own.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Apart from the advertising data that Google AdMob collects (section 5), we do not share your data with anyone.

8. Deleting your data and account

  • No account in the current version: the current version of the app does not let you create an account, so none of your data is stored on our servers.
  • Local data: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in phone settings (Settings → Apps → Noor-e-Islam → Storage → Clear data), removes everything stored on your phone.
  • Advertising data: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads), as described in section 5.
  • By email: for any data request, write to fshaida96@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

We keep account data only while your account exists. Advertising data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described in section 5.

9. Children

Noor-e-Islam is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child's data has reached us, contact us at fshaida96@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

11. Contact

Email: fshaida96@gmail.com
